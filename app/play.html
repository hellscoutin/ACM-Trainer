                How it works

                Select a body part on the diagram, choose a category on the left, then an action. Actions take their in-game time and are listed only when they can be done, as in game. Feel Pulse, head tilt-chin lift, BVM and CPR continue until you stop them. Vital signs are hidden unless an examination or a pulse oximeter shows them. Space pauses; the speed buttons fast-forward time. Hints (top bar) suggest the next steps: Basic uses only what the menu and devices show, Intermediate adds your examination findings, Advanced sees the true patient state. Runs with hints are marked in your records.
